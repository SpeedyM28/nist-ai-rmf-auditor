# ACME Corp AI Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for the responsible development and use of artificial intelligence systems at ACME Corp.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who develop, deploy, or use AI systems on behalf of ACME Corp.

## 3. Principles
We are committed to developing AI that is:
- Fair and unbiased
- Transparent and explainable
- Safe and secure
- Privacy-preserving

## 4. Governance
The Chief Technology Officer is responsible for overseeing AI initiatives. The AI Governance Committee reviews all high-risk AI systems before deployment.

## 5. Development Standards
All AI systems must be validated before deployment. Testing data should be representative of production environments. Security reviews are required.

## 6. Monitoring
AI systems shall be monitored for performance drift. Incidents must be reported to the security team.

## 7. Third-Party AI
Third-party AI systems must be reviewed before integration.
